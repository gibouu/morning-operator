Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent raster sun artwork for a calm iPhone morning app, composited over warm cream paper.
Primary request: Take the visual qualities of a natural photographed sunset sun and translate that sun into extremely fine photographic halftone / stochastic stipple art. Create one isolated luminous solar disc, centered in a square image, occupying about 62% of the canvas diameter, with generous transparent space around it. This is a new original sun asset, not a UI mockup.
Subject and light: A real atmospheric sun seen through very soft sunset haze. Subtle organic tonal bands within the sun suggest thin airborne haze, without literal scenery. A pale creamy light core transitions through honey, dusty apricot, copper and quiet rust at the lower rim. Light is soft and luminous. The outer edge is feathered and dissolves through finer and sparser impressions into genuine alpha transparency; no visible hard circle outline.
Medium and grain: Delicate high-resolution photogravure / fine-grain risograph impression. Thousands of tiny, almost sand-like irregular halftone ink dots, approximately 1–2 pixels at 1024px output. The texture should read as soft photographic grain at a glance, with individual microscopic stipples visible only close up. Organic stochastic distribution; no straight rows, no large circles, no giant polka dots, no geometric dot-grid logo.
Composition and alpha: Only the sun and its very subtle feathered luminous particulate edge. Background fully transparent, including all corners. No baked paper rectangle, no white matte, no checkerboard pattern painted into the image, no horizon, sea, landscape, clouds outside the sun, rays, or cast shadow. At least 15% clear margin on every side. Preserve true transparent alpha for paper compositing.
Palette: Warm cream, pale ivory light, muted golden apricot, burnt orange and rust. No purple, violet, magenta, blue or black. No text, letters, numbers, UI, icon frame, watermark or border.
