# Morning observation and evidence plan

Updated 10 October 2026. This is the implementation plan for [#19 — explicitly started observation sessions and learning windows](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/19) and [#21 — evidence-based daily and weekly analytics](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/21). Source capability, documented Apple APIs and hardware verification are separate. See [PHASE-ONE.md](PHASE-ONE.md) for actual results and [CAPABILITIES.md](CAPABILITIES.md) for the broader architecture.

## Product outcome

The iPhone is the sole active product client; the Mac remains a development/test host and optional controller. The person chooses **Figure out my morning routine**, authorizes a finite learning period, and goes through their morning. Morning Operator learns the order, duration, repetition and purpose of activities from available evidence, then proposes a routine the person can correct conversationally and edit. The goal is to remove repetitive handling while preserving enjoyable rituals, including entertainment, social browsing and chess. A shorter morning is a preference to negotiate, not a reason to discard the activities the person values.

The intended learning period is a person-selected **three or seven mornings**, with selected days and a morning window covering the desired first one to two hours. That is the product observation window; it is not a claim that the prototype captures two uninterrupted hours. The current bounded recorder covers short segments, and every uncovered interval remains unknown. Earlier history, when actually returned through authorized Screen Time access, can seed aggregate suggestions. It does not replace semantic observation or establish a complete routine. Any initial/update proposal shows its actual sample size and exceptions; three days is a product starting point, not a validated accuracy threshold. Incomplete days do not silently count as fully observed mornings.

Learning, recording and running the approved routine are distinct modes. Starting a learning window does not start an approved routine, and a model proposal does not authorize an external action. Record whether evidence came from the person's own behavior or from a test/automated routine so the system does not learn its own playback as a new human habit.

## The intended observation journey

### 1. Choose a finite learning window

Ask for the days, local time zone, earliest/latest morning times, learning end date and preferred segment limit. Explain which evidence sources are enabled and what visible information recording may capture. Keep camera and microphone off for this path. Offer Stop learning and a way to exclude or delete an observation. Source-specific permissions are requested when that source is enabled, with a usable manual start when another source is unavailable.

Saving the schedule enables a start opportunity. It does not silently authorize a new screen stream each morning. Keep consent scope and revision, selected sources, configured window and expiry with the observation metadata. If the person travels or changes the schedule, make the effective time zone visible and preserve each completed morning's original time zone.

### 2. Offer a brief start at the right moment

The preferred native spike is a repeating DeviceActivity morning interval. Apple documents `DeviceActivityMonitor.intervalDidStart(for:)` as being called when the person first uses the device within the interval. Test whether the configured extension can record that callback and present an appropriate start affordance on the owner's setup. It is a **first device use within the configured interval** signal, not proof of waking, a physical lift, the first use of the entire day, or permission to begin capture without the picker. [Apple interval-start semantics](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitymonitor/intervaldidstart(for:)).

The implemented one-time invitation is a smaller, separate routine-entry slice: it uses an explicit future time, a fixed absolute instant and a person's notification tap. It does not schedule a week of observation or invoke the recorder. Its simulator result does not validate DeviceActivity or an alarm trigger. [Morning-entry guide](MORNING-ENTRY.md).

A concise affordance such as **Learn this morning** should open the start flow. Manual entry remains available. A scheduled notification or explicitly installed Shortcut can be an alternative, with notification/automation behavior verified independently. Do not describe a notification as guaranteed delivery or assume an extension can force the foreground app open. Prevent repeated prompts for the same scheduled window once accepted, dismissed or expired.

Record the distinctions: configured window start, callback received, prompt shown if known, user-requested start, acknowledged recording start, recording end and routine-run start. An alarm, wake estimate, Screen Time first-use signal and physical pickup are different events.

### 3. Start visibly, then get out of the way

After the person taps Start, present Apple's screen-sharing picker. Only after the recording output acknowledges capture should the app-owned recorder/Learn sheets close and the app show compact status. This does not establish that Apple's separate system panel closes automatically; test that behavior independently and do not assume an undocumented dismissal API. Keep a stable app-wide owner so dismissing a sheet cannot destroy the recording operation. The person can return to Home or another app normally; there is no promise that this app can arbitrarily launch or drive that app at runtime.

Show an in-app active recording status with elapsed time, selected limit and Stop, alongside the system's recording indication. Provide a clear return path to status and a ready-to-review result. A future Live Activity is an optional separately implemented surface, not the only way to stop. Starting, stopping and an uncertain stop must remain distinguishable from stopped.

The full-display stream's background support depends on the iOS 27 ScreenCaptureKit path and `screen-capture` background mode. Verify it with the system picker, app switching, locking, interruption and system stop on the actual phone. No microphone or camera capture is needed for this journey. [Apple's iOS screen-capture sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit/capturing-screen-content-on-ios).

### 4. Stop with a real bound

The intended stop rule is the earliest of the chosen elapsed duration, configured window end, file-size bound, explicit user/system stop or capture failure. Use a monotonic clock for elapsed recording time and a separate wall-clock deadline for the scheduled window. Record requested stop, confirmed stream stop and finalized file separately. A wall-clock change must not reset an elapsed-duration budget.

`intervalDidEnd(for:)` is called on first device use outside the scheduled interval or when monitoring stops; it is not a guaranteed callback at the desired cutoff. It must not be the only stop mechanism. If shutdown is uncertain, keep the capture resources and visible retry/status control until the state is resolved. Do not analyze or delete a file as if it were final while the stream may still be writing it. [Apple interval-end semantics](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitymonitor/intervaldidend(for:)).

An optional future **suggest stopping after 15–20 minutes of apparent inactivity** policy is an inference. An unchanged frame may be reading; changing video may be unattended playback; a stale usage query may mean delayed data. No current source proves person inactivity. Keep this policy separately labeled, configurable and subordinate to the explicit duration/window bounds. ScreenCaptureKit's idle frame status means the display did not change; it does not mean the person stopped using the phone.

### 5. Review and learn after the morning

On return, show what was observed, how much was analyzed, the uncovered intervals and the proposed activities. Local extraction and model preparation can happen after the explicitly authorized recording completes; review remains required before approving the proposed routine. Allow corrections such as “that was work,” “I only skim those,” “keep my chess time,” and “this morning was unusual.” Retain the correction's provenance rather than overwriting the source observation.

Offer a day timeline and swipeable history. The seven-day view should expose comparable-day counts, known durations, missing observations and repeated app visits. Compare a future assisted routine against a separately observed baseline; planned minutes and a Morning Operator run's elapsed time are not proof of total phone time or time saved.

## Complementary evidence sources

| Source | What it can contribute | Limits and status |
|---|---|---|
| Explicit screen recording | Timestamped visible content; inferred app/screen changes and activity sequences | Picker consent; protected/blank content and gaps; no system-wide touch log or guaranteed app identifier in iOS frames. Current sparse OCR is insufficient for exact rapid interaction counts. |
| Optional per-app Shortcuts automations | Events when a selected app is opened/switched to or closed/switched away from | User installs/configures the automations. Logger data logic has focused checks; installed delivery remains unverified; supports Instagram/TikTok/Snapchat only. Timestamp is callback execution time, potentially delayed. |
| DeviceActivity first-use callback | A first-use-in-scheduled-window signal | Extension/authorization/profile work and hardware verification pending; no silent recording or physical-lift sensor. |
| Entitled DeviceActivity data access | System-reported activity durations, pickup-related fields and freshness metadata for returned intervals | iOS 26.4+ capability, authorization and regional/distribution conditions below; aggregate data, not an ordered tap/open/switch stream. |
| Reviewed user correction | Meaning, preference and contextual exceptions | Label as user-reported/corrected, not a measured interaction. |
| Tethered XCTest | Known issued test actions and observed postconditions on a specific target | Separate development controller. Never mix its synthetic navigation with natural personal habit evidence. |

### App-entry logging for cycles

Apple's Shortcuts App trigger fires on opening or switching to a selected app, and on closing or switching away. The new `LogAppTransitionIntent` source declares background execution, accepts a supported app, transition and optional event UUID, records callback time/time zone and persists through `AppTransitionLogStore` without requesting foreground UI. Review displays Today entry counts and returns after another tracked app. Source support is limited to Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat; the app provides setup instructions rather than installing automations. Foreground start/capture intents remain separate. Background delivery, cold launch, locked-device behavior, duplicates, delayed delivery and actual process/store behavior still need validation. The journal is separate from `workspace.json`; its actor serializes same-process writes. A supplied stable UUID supports retries, while an omitted ID creates a new event. This is not a guarantee that independently retried Shortcuts actions will automatically share an ID. [Apple App-trigger definitions](https://support.apple.com/guide/shortcuts/setting-triggers-apde31e9638b/ios).

Call these **observed app entries/returns** rather than process launches. The sequence among configured apps may omit an unconfigured app in between. Treat an unmatched close/open interval as unknown; do not infer contiguous use or precise duration from a missing event. This setup is optional: a person who does not install the automations can still teach through recording.

### Native usage and pickup aggregates

Do not carry forward the older blanket assumption that Screen Time data can never leave a report extension. iOS 26.4+ documents `DeviceActivityData.activityData(filteredBy:using:)`, requiring the **Family Controls App and Website Usage** capability and approved data-access authorization. Apple documents development/testing support in any region; customer use is limited to an EU location and EU Apple Account. The required Apple-provided development profile and device authorization must be established before this prototype can claim access. [Activity-data API and availability conditions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/activitydata(filteredby:using:)).

Only one app at a time can hold `.approvedWithDataAccess`; granting it to another app replaces the prior holder's authorization. Explain this before connecting. Treat rejection, revocation or replacement as unavailable evidence. Ordinary authorization uses opaque app/domain representations; the additional authorization enables non-tokenized data. The normal DeviceActivityReport extension still prevents network requests and moving sensitive content outside its address space; it is not a substitute export path. [Data-access authorization](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/familycontrols/authorizationstatus/approvedwithdataaccess), [DeviceActivityReport](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivityreport).

Relevant documented fields include an activity segment's `firstPickup`, `totalPickupsWithoutApplicationActivity`, date interval and duration, and each application's duration, `numberOfPickups` and notifications. Per-app pickups are direct-to-app pickups, not all app opens or switches. No ordered foreground-switch list or app-launch count is provided by this aggregate model. Present Apple-reported pickup metrics using their defined scope; do not rebrand them as a verified count of physical phone lifts. [First pickup](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/activitysegment/firstpickup), [Pickups without app activity](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/activitysegment/totalpickupswithoutapplicationactivity), [Per-app pickups](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/applicationactivity/numberofpickups).

Notification counts are not unread-message/email counts and include no message-content guarantee. There is no historical raw-tap feed in these aggregate structures; ordinary UIKit touch handling receives events in the app's own view/window. A first pickup timestamp can describe returned usage, not prove an alarm time or wake intent. [Application activity fields](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/applicationactivity), [UIKit touch delivery](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiresponder/touchesbegan(_:with:)).

Store the query interval/filter, device scope and `lastUpdatedDate`. Even `.live` may return recent cached data. A missing/old result is not zero activity or a reliable stop signal. Do not assign hourly/daily aggregates to a narrower morning interval without supporting interval data. Do not add native durations to video estimates for the same period; reconcile or display their different scopes. [Live policy](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/policy/live), [Freshness](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/deviceactivity/deviceactivitydata/lastupdateddate).

The next bootstrap spike should query the chosen three/seven local days, preserve returned intervals and device scope, and show eligible/missing days before suggesting anything. Repeated cached/live reads replace the same metric revision rather than adding duplicate activity. Keep zones and sources distinct, including daylight-saving boundaries. Returned aggregate duration can support a planned allocation; semantic order/purpose still needs reviewed observations or explicit user input. Historical coverage, retention and refresh behavior must be measured on the authorized device before promising an immediate three-day baseline.

### Optional stronger analysis and advance preparation

Current analysis stays on device; no cloud request or upload was introduced by this plan. iOS 27's direct PCC SDK and Shortcuts' separate Cloud/Cloud Pro actions are documented options, with different eligibility/runtime checks. Any future cloud trial requires explicit consent to the source scope and the managed SDK entitlement where applicable; no silent fallback. Bounded ordered images/OCR can be evaluated, but a larger context cannot fill gaps in a recording. See [documented model paths](CAPABILITIES.md#planned-apple-api-paths--checked-10-october-2026).

Learning a routine and preparing tomorrow's changing content are separate jobs. The target preparation window may be 4 a.m. or three hours before a user-supplied wake schedule. iOS chooses background runtime, so prepare opportunistically, retain a bounded expiring source-backed snapshot and refresh changes on wake/start. Unconnected email/news/social sources stay unavailable. This lifecycle is planned; today's Calendar/Weather preparation is an in-memory implementation. [Planned briefing lifecycle](ARCHITECTURE.md#planned-ahead-of-time-briefing-lifecycle), [Apple scheduling guarantee](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/backgroundtasks/bgtaskrequest/earliestbegindate).

## Metric definitions and truthfulness

| Metric | Rule |
|---|---|
| Capture duration | Measured from acknowledged capture and confirmed termination. A requested limit is configuration; interrupted/unresolved capture remains incomplete. |
| Morning Operator run duration | Session-event timing with pauses separated. It measures an app session; interrupted timing stays unknown. Keep planned duration separate. |
| App visible at a sampled moment | Record the timestamp, identified app if supported, method and confidence. A screenshot alone need not establish the exact bundle identifier. |
| App entry/return | A source-supported entry event, or an explicitly inferred visual transition. Multiple screens inside one app are not new entries. Deduplicate repeated evidence. |
| App switch | Ordered evidence that the foreground context changed. A gap may hide other switches; a sparse A/B pair does not prove a direct A-to-B transition. |
| Return cycle | An explicitly defined return to an anchor app after at least one evidenced departure, within the same eligible window. A→B→A is one return to A; A→unknown→A is uncertain, not an exact cycle. Store the sequence, time span, source and coverage. |
| Repeated pattern | Compare similar ordered sequences across comparable mornings. Keep overlapping loops and repeated visits distinct; summing overlapping pattern matches must not inflate the count of mornings or elapsed time. |
| Pickups | Use the native aggregate's exact field definition and returned scope when available. App openings, recording starts and visual changes are not substitute pickup counts. |
| Swipes, reels and likes | Sparse video can support labeled estimates or visible state changes. It does not establish every gesture, distinct reel watched or successful like. An automated swipe is a controller action, not a human swipe. |
| Email/message handling | Visible text is not proof of reading; a compose screen is not sent; disappearance is not deletion. “No reply observed in this recording” is narrower than “no replies that morning.” |
| Activity intent | Ask for correction or label inference. Work/leisure, skimming/reading and enjoyment are not directly measured from a frame. |

For every value distinguish **measured/system-reported**, **inferred**, **user-corrected** and **unknown**. A confidence score is not a measured count. A proven lower bound can be shown as “at least,” with its evidence; an estimate must stay labeled. Zero is valid only when the source actually supports zero for the specified interval and metric. Missing fields remain absent, never silently zero.

## Timestamped evidence and persistence contract

The table below remains the broader integration contract. App-transition history/journal and pure `ObservationTrace` / comparable-day reports now exist, but they do not implement scheduled-window consent, recording-to-trace adaptation, persistent source retention or runtime multi-day integration. [Observation metrics](OBSERVATION-METRICS.md) specifies the implemented trace, unknowns, visible-visit lower bounds and sampled-time estimates. Evolve the existing core history model without creating a competing day/week store.

| Record | Required content |
|---|---|
| Learning window | Stable ID; configured UTC bounds and local time zone; selected days; consent scope/version/expiry; enabled sources; actual trigger and callback time; lifecycle state. |
| Recording segment | Window ID; source ID; requested/acknowledged start; requested/confirmed stop; finalized status; stop reason; monotonic elapsed duration; available media-time mapping; coverage ranges and gaps. |
| Evidence item | Stable ID; source kind; actual media presentation timestamp or source event timestamp; wall time only when known; extractor/version; app identity/confidence if available; retention state and reviewed excerpt/reference. |
| Observation event | App visible/entry/exit/return or semantic candidate; evidence IDs; inferred/measured label; event time and received time where distinct; human/test/automation origin. |
| Metric result | Definition/version; interval/device scope; value or unknown; unit; count/denominator; source and confidence/limitations; whether a lower bound, estimate or complete supported value. |
| Review correction | Original evidence/claim IDs; corrected interpretation; author/source and time; exclusion/unusual-day flag; revision lineage. |

Media presentation times must not be fabricated into wall-clock timestamps for imported recordings that have no trustworthy capture-start mapping. Preserve original source times and receipt times; do not silently sort delayed callbacks into a false exact sequence. Split local days using calendar/time-zone rules, including daylight-saving changes, rather than assuming every day has 86,400 seconds.

Use stable source identities and idempotent imports. Reanalyzing the same recording must replace or revise derived observations, not double the counts. The existing `DailyAppObservation` is an authoritative app/day snapshot; build it from reconciled evidence and upsert that snapshot. Keep provenance and conflicting interpretations available for review instead of adding each reprocessing result as new behavior.

No model/OCR text may instruct the app to perform an action or change consent. Visible content is untrusted evidence. Structured extraction and routine proposals must pass validation, and action dispatch remains controlled by the approved operation and session state.

## Coverage, day/week summaries and retention

**Captured coverage and analyzed coverage are different.** A continuous ten-minute recording analyzed at 24 sampled moments leaves roughly 26 seconds between moments. Those sample points do not prove what happened throughout each intervening interval. Track failed frames, blank/protected frames, omitted spans, denied sources and source outages. Never interpret an unchanged/idle frame as confirmed absence of user activity.

Seven-day summaries should show the seven selected local dates, days with evidence, windows attempted/completed, known duration/count denominators and uncertainty. For example, an average based on three days of known app-duration evidence is a three-day average, not a seven-day average with four implicit zeros. Distinguish a day with no observation from a fully observed zero. Exclude synthetic fixtures by default. Keep weekday/weekend and user-marked unusual mornings separate when comparability matters.

Retain only the evidence needed for the chosen learning purpose. The recovery contract retains app-owned raw video after OCR failure, cancellation or failure to persist extracted evidence, and exposes Retry/Discard. Remove it after the archive/workspace save or explicit discard; later model failure does not require raw-media retention. A user-imported original is preserved. The local RecordingRecovery slot holds one pending recording and offers it again after launch, without automatically retrying analysis. Broad physical recovery remains separate from the recorded storage/import checks. Discard recording and Clear this observation affect separate stored data: the latter removes the selected acquisition, including original/corrected text and attached interpretation, and invalidates a dependent pending routine update. Pending typed citations disappear on approval, discard or invalidation; existing routine/history quotations remain separately saved. A future timeline must record when source media has been deleted and must not offer nonexistent frame links. Optional longer raw-media retention and expiry are future controls. The transition setup sheet offers Clear recorded app activity; its device flow still needs verification. Journal export and automatic expiry are not implemented. Disabling Shortcuts automations stops future callbacks but does not delete saved events.

Coordinate with [#8 — retention, export and forgetting](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/8). Provide reviewed local export and deletion by recording, morning and all history; explain which external originals remain untouched. Keep personal recordings, screenshots, OCR and test result bundles out of Git. No recording, private message, HealthKit data or derived behavioral profile is a monetization feed in this prototype.

## Controlled ground truth before broader learning

Tracked in [#23 — Validate recorded mornings against controlled ground-truth phone actions](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/23).

The immediate quality gap is grounded activity interpretation: a real clip proved visible saved text and draft display, yet setup/marketing copy became proposed habits. A deterministic guard rejects the known input; it is not proof of general recognition or real-model abstention. Preserve the earlier empty-Learn XCTest/user failures and the later one-clip success separately in [PHASE-ONE](PHASE-ONE.md). The [recorded benchmark](OBSERVATION-BENCHMARK.md) now covers controlled actions in two native fixture apps, actual simulator movie extraction and saved-evidence review. The [media/trace adapter](OBSERVATION-METRICS.md) is implemented; unavailable Mac inference and the unsupported simulator backend still prevent a semantic-accuracy result. Continue checking every boundary—finalized media, decoded frames, OCR, saved observation, displayed field and model output—rather than accepting a success banner.

Extend the versioned benchmark using the actual capture pipeline. Predeclare a sequence of known app openings/returns, a small number of scrolls, and selected synthetic email/message items with recognizable text. An owner or the bounded development harness performs the script. Log issued actions, observed postconditions and timestamped human annotations separately: an issued swipe does not prove the screen moved, and opening an email does not prove the person read or understood it. Ordinary phone execution and the test harness must use the same extraction path; fixture text must not bypass capture while claiming an end-to-end pass.

Compare what the recording actually contains, what OCR extracts and what the model claims. Report per-category true positives, false positives and missed evidenced events; precision is true positives divided by all claimed positives, and recall is true positives divided by all annotated positives within the evaluated scope. An empty denominator is unknown/not applicable, not perfect accuracy. Match events one-to-one within a declared time tolerance so duplicate claims cannot inflate a score. Report unsupported assertions, deliberate unknowns/abstentions, excluded intervals, failed samples and timing/count errors separately. Distinguish capture coverage from extraction/model accuracy: events outside the capture scope cannot become successful observations, but their absence must remain visible in the end-to-end result.

Include rapid transitions, static reading, repeated frames, protected/blank content, missing callbacks and cancellation/relaunch cases. Keep a held-out script for regression so changing a prompt does not merely memorize the example. Use synthetic email content initially; any later personal item must be deliberately selected, stay local and retain no unnecessary third-party content. Choose success thresholds from the measured pilot and write them down before expanding claims; no accuracy target is reported as achieved yet.

Once single-session evidence is useful, collect four or five adequately observed comparable mornings for initial estimates. Show the number of mornings contributing to each average, duration range and repeated sequence. A five-day calendar period with two usable observations is a two-observation estimate; a partial morning may support one metric while another stays unknown. The existing seven-day review remains a calendar display, not a reason to insert missing days as zero. Label scripted benchmark mornings as synthetic, separate from genuine personal averages.

## The second half: narrate, act, then learn again

Learning a routine is only the first half of the product. After the person approves a plan, the assistant should explain the selected current screen in speech or text, surface the relevant information and carry out the supported part of the morning. The next development slice belongs to #7, with voice from #6 and durable effects from #12: acquire a fresh observation, narrate only supported visible facts, resolve one scoped action, execute through the actual available adapter/controller, then acquire new evidence and verify the result. Reading an instruction aloud or issuing a tap is not completed work by itself. Keep screen narration separate from speculative statements about the person's intent.

Start with a short, selected read-only screen sequence and a supported action. The tethered Mac test controller remains a development execution option even when the separate native Mac app is not installed. Native app actions, deliberately configured Shortcuts and a tethered controller have different capabilities; report which performed each step. Preserve the stop/cancel path and current approved routine, reject stale or ambiguous targets, and route external writes through the existing scoped approval and outcome journal. Do not promise unrestricted standalone iPhone control.

Continue learning after each eligible, consented morning rather than freezing the profile after onboarding. Keep actor/source labels for human-led, assistant-executed, mixed and synthetic/test segments. Exclude the assistant's own repeated playback from evidence of a newly preferred human habit; explicit user corrections and requested changes can still update preferences. Retain the prior estimate, sample sizes, evidence and reason for a proposed change. Four/five-day estimates can initialize the profile, while subsequent comparable observations update it with transparent coverage and exceptional-day handling. New material routine versions remain reviewable, active sessions stay pinned and unsupported actions do not become authorized through frequency alone.

Offer habit reduction as an optional preference: the person chooses which activity, an achievable target and a time period, or chooses no reduction. Preserve chosen enjoyment and required rituals. Acknowledge a missed target neutrally; do not silently impose a shield or erase entertainment. Compare against measured eligible baseline days and label estimated changes. Stop/end the experiment when requested or when its selected period expires. Screen Time enforcement, if desired, is separate #17 work; the baseline experience can simply show progress and suggest adjustments.

## Later research-data hypothesis: separate consent and evidence

A possible future business is a deliberately contributed dataset of mobile interaction recordings and aligned actions/outcomes for mobile-agent research. This is a research hypothesis, not a validated market, valuation or current collection program. It differs from personal routine inference and from aggregate screen-time analytics. No current recovery recording, saved OCR or activity journal becomes research data automatically.

Investigate this in [#24 — Evaluate a separately opted-in mobile-agent research dataset](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/24), alongside #8 and #18. Define a narrow useful task/data schema with potential research users, using synthetic examples first. If a real-data pilot is subsequently chosen, participation must be separately opted into for a named purpose and independently usable without joining. People select the recordings, review redaction and the proposed contribution, and understand retention, permitted recipients/uses and withdrawal limits before any export. Test redaction against message bodies, contacts, account identifiers, credentials, notifications and incidental third-party information; redacted is not automatically anonymous. Exclude sensitive content categories from an initial pilot and determine applicable rights/platform requirements before selecting data or recipients.

The feasibility decision should weigh demand, action/observation alignment, annotation quality, coverage, redaction failure rates, cost and participant value. Design auditable export/deletion and stop-participation controls, including clear limits after a separately authorized external release. The result may be proceed, narrow or stop. This plan does not lengthen raw retention, enable background data collection, upload recordings, contact research buyers, license data or authorize a sale. Current raw retention remains only what is needed for explicit recording recovery.

## Delivery slices and acceptance evidence

### #19: observation lifecycle and start experience

1. Saving a finite schedule does not begin screen capture. Manual start works without DeviceActivity; unavailable/revoked permissions produce an honest fallback.
2. The first-use callback spike records configured interval, callback time and observed phone behavior. Demonstrate repeated-day, no-use, already-in-use, time-zone and out-of-window cases before calling the trigger reliable. Keep physical pickup/wake claims out of the result.
3. Picker confirmation starts recording; acknowledged capture closes the sheet; the person can use another app; a stable owner and visible Stop/status remain. Cancellation and returning to the app do not accidentally restart recording.
4. Test each selected time bound, file-size stop, explicit/system stop, background/lock behavior, late start completion and uncertain stop. Record actual stop/finalization times and overshoot. No incomplete file is treated as final and no active capture loses ownership when its view disappears.
5. A completed recording produces visible nonempty grounded evidence, persists it and restores it after relaunch, or shows an actionable extraction failure. An Observation loaded banner with an empty Learn field fails this criterion. Discard/cleanup and retry do not reuse unrelated personal media. Window expiry and Stop learning disable future start opportunities.

### #21: trustworthy analytics and cycles

1. Connect the implemented pure trace/report types to timestamped extraction with stable evidence IDs, source retention state and separate captured/analyzed coverage; populate the existing daily model only from validated reviewed evidence.
2. Verify deliberate A→B→A, A→unknown→A, repeated same-app frames, rapid switches, delayed/duplicate logger callbacks and repeated imports. Exact, inferred and unknown outcomes must differ as specified above.
3. Validate installed delivery of the implemented selected-app Shortcuts logger, journal and Review activity view, then extend coordinated persistence/coverage as needed. Confirm it does not bring Morning Operator forward or record its own activity as the person's natural app sequence.
4. Spike the newer DeviceActivity data-access entitlement/profile/authorization on the owner's device. Record availability, returned field semantics, device/interval scope and freshness. An entitlement failure is a documented limitation, not a zero-usage morning.
5. Validate zero versus unknown, three-known-days versus seven calendar days, overlapping evidence, daylight-saving boundaries, unusual mornings, synthetic exclusions, corrections, deletion and export.
6. Use the controlled ground-truth benchmark above to evaluate transition and semantic estimates, including precision/recall, unsupported claims, unknowns, duplicate matching and coverage. Validate four/five-day estimates with real denominators before claiming useful personal averages or proposing V2.

Coordinate [#9 — time-linked evidence](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/9), [#10 — enjoyable rituals](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/10) and [#11 — multi-day versions](https://github.com/gibouu/morning-operator/issues/11) around the same evidence IDs and correction semantics. ## Technical questions to close with evidence

- Can the DeviceActivity first-use callback reliably offer a brief start on this signed personal build, with which authorization, extension configuration and notification behavior?
- Can the newer usage-data entitlement be provisioned for this development setup, and which useful interval/device scopes and refresh delays are actually returned?
- Can selected-app Shortcuts log without a foreground switch, with durable writes across process boundaries and defensible event ordering?
- How much on-device extraction is needed to detect useful transitions without excessive battery, heat or retained private content? The current 24-frame OCR pass cannot answer this for a long morning.
- How do lock, protected content, interruption, background execution and clock changes affect the chosen recording bounds and file completion on this iOS version?
- Which desired metrics can be measured, which require labeled estimates, and which should remain absent? Physical lifts, exact touch history and reading intent must not acquire invented certainty.
- What minimum comparable coverage justifies a new routine version, and which preferences should survive sparse or contradictory observations?

These are development spikes, not public-distribution gates. Preserve the owner's personal proof-of-concept ambition while recording exactly what each native path and tethered experiment establishes.
