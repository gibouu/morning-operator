# MORE free entry and optional subscription

The owner selected **€4.99 per month with a one-week free trial** on 16 October 2026, then explicitly made app entry free in ordinary Debug and Release builds, including submission. The entry flow explains learning, reviewing and delegating the morning and offers **Continue for free / Continuer gratuitement** from the introduction and the offer. Actual purchase prices and trial eligibility come from StoreKit. There are no permission requests or paid visual-theme features in this flow.

## Native flow and setup handoff

`MorningOperatorApp` presents `MorningSubscriptionEntryView`. It shows discovery immediately while StoreKit loads. Choosing free entry records `more.entry.hasEnteredApp` and opens `MorningRootView` with its normal optional permission/routine setup. Verified subscribers also retain this entry choice. The preference persists across relaunch and updates, remains separate from subscription rights, and never grants a transaction or an iPhone permission. Debug UI tests scope it to their workspace. Settings provides **Membership & purchases / Abonnement et achats** to reopen the offer, purchase or restore, and return to Settings.

`MorningWelcomeFlow` owns the three introduction pages and offer presentation. `MorningSubscriptionStore` loads the configured monthly auto-renewable product, checks exact one-week trial eligibility, verifies purchases, listens for transaction changes, restores through `AppStore.sync()` and checks expiration. Verified billing grace is honored when Apple supplies a future grace expiry. A changed price or trial requires another tap after the offer updates. Pending, cancelled, missing, failed or unverified purchases never create subscription rights and never disable free continuation. A declined family purchase request has an explicit retry, which reconciles verified access before another purchase request. Expiration and revocation update billing rights without interrupting ordinary app access or an active morning. Privacy controls remain accessible from the offer.

All four screens use the Home landscape and clock without location permission. Frosted semantic surfaces, system typography and native Liquid Glass controls follow the app design system; day/night remains independent of subscription state. Reading surfaces use native regular material; Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast retain opaque reading surfaces. The light, dark and tinted app icons retain the approved dotted sunrise, regenerated with `scripts/make-app-icon.swift`. Accessibility text sizes move the action area into the scroll view and page changes reset its position. The existing privacy-data screen follows the same solar appearance. The separate access setup reuses these same native colors, typography and primary buttons, while retaining its own explanations, sequence and permission policy.

## App Store configuration still required

The default configured product identifier is `com.gibouu.more.monthly`, overridable with the `MORE_SUBSCRIPTION_PRODUCT_ID` Xcode build setting. It is a configuration contract, not evidence that an App Store product exists. Create or select the actual auto-renewable monthly subscription in App Store Connect, set its French price to €4.99 and its introductory free trial to one week, and use its real identifier in the build. Complete Apple's agreements, availability and review requirements and the app's published privacy policy before release. The app never silently substitutes a fixed marketing price if the product is unavailable.

`Configuration/MORE.storekit` is an opt-in **local test fixture**: €4.99 monthly, one-week free trial, French storefront. It is bundled only in the UI-test target. The ordinary run scheme does not activate a simulated store or grant trial access. `MorningSubscriptionPurchaseTests` starts its own `SKTestSession` against this fixture. A DEBUG-only `--preview-onboarding` renders fixed sample pricing and simulated purchase failures. It has a nonvisual test identifier and no visible preview badge; purchase and restore never fabricate success. Its explicit DEBUG-only “Skip for now” uses the same existing local developer-access preference as the ordinary Debug cross, exits the preview into the unchanged real workspace and shows one overview of the supported real iPhone accesses. Accept then requests them sequentially, starting with Screen Time when available, while Not now skips the requests. It grants no StoreKit transaction or system permission and never resets existing iOS authorization. Health remains a separate real Settings connection. Release has no preview skip. Other existing `--ui-testing` journeys bypass the gate only in DEBUG. `--ui-testing-real-subscription` explicitly exercises the real coordinator against the local StoreKit session.

The dedicated `MORESubscriptions` scheme activates the fixture for manual runs and selects only `MorningSubscriptionPurchaseTests` for testing. The initial CLI run on iOS 26.5 Simulator (23F77) failed before the purchase assertions: `SKTestSession` could not save its configuration or apply overrides (`SKInternalErrorDomain Code=3`). Apple confirms the matching issue in [FB22237318](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/826971); [iOS 26.6 release notes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/ios-ipados-release-notes/ios-ipados-26_6-release-notes) list the Simulator connection fix as FB22500243. The native StoreKit tests explicitly skip on iOS 26.5 Simulator and retain their assertions on other runtimes. A skip is unverified purchase behavior, not a passing purchase test. Use iOS 26.6 or later for the native purchase proof.

## Verification

Generate the project with `scripts/generate-project.sh`. Build and run `MorningWelcomeFlowTests` and `MorningSubscriptionPurchaseTests` on a dedicated simulator. `MO_ONBOARDING_SCREENSHOT_DIR` exports synthetic review images outside Git. Local StoreKit results do not establish a live App Store product, sandbox payment, physical-device purchase or production entitlement behavior. Record observed results in the delivery PR.

## Automatic interface language

At first launch, the primary iPhone language alone selects French (`fr`, including regional variants) or English (every other language). Secondary French preferences never select French for a non-French primary language. No language picker or questionnaire precedes discovery. Settings later offers exactly Français and English; a saved choice takes precedence on subsequent launches. The root applies the chosen locale and left-to-right layout to the interface. Regional formatting and the actual App Store price remain sourced from the device and StoreKit. Shared `AppLanguage` localization selects the intended resource language for native messages as well as the SwiftUI interface.
